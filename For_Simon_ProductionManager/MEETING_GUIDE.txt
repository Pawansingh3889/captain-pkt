FOR SIMON (Production Manager)
================================

PURPOSE: Get his support before the Danny meeting

WHEN: Before Monday meeting — during a quiet moment on the floor

WHAT TO SAY:
"Simon, I have been working on a proposal to automate some
of the reporting we do in Excel — plan attainment, yield
tracking. I have a meeting with Danny on Monday. Would you
be supportive if I worked on this during quieter periods?"

WHY SIMON MATTERS:
- He currently does plan attainment manually in Excel
- Your proposal automates his work — he needs to see this
  as helping him, not replacing him
- If he supports you, Danny is more likely to approve
- He controls your shift schedule — you need his flexibility

DOCUMENT:
- Proposal_Short_Version.docx — show him the one-pager
- Emphasise: "This automates the Excel work you do every day.
  You would get the same data without spending 30 minutes
  compiling it."

DO NOT:
- Make him feel his job is threatened
- Go over his head without telling him first
- Ask for time off — ask for flexibility during quiet periods
